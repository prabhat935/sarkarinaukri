Using the Countdown Well

  • Apply early, not on the last day. Official websites slow down near the deadline, and payment failures or a rejected photo can cost you the chance. Aim to finish a week before the last date, and make sure your photo and signature are the right size before you start.
  • Check eligibility before you pay. Work out your age on the notice's cut-off date with the age eligibility calculator.
  • Plan revision backwards from the exam date. With 30 days left, most candidates do best moving from learning new topics to full-length mock tests and revision.
  • Watch for the admit card. Many commissions release it only a few days before the exam, and sometimes only a city slip first. Keep your registration number and password safe.

Dates on this page come from the notices behind our posts; we do not list exams we have not checked. For anything else, the commission's own website is the place to confirm a date.
