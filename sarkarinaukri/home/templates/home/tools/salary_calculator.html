How the Salary Is Worked Out

  • Basic pay: new joiners start at the first cell of their level in the 7th CPC pay matrix, and rise by about 3% at each annual increment.
  • DA: basic x the DA rate. It is revised every January and July.
  • HRA: 30%, 20% or 10% of basic for X, Y and Z cities. These rates apply because DA has passed 50%. No HRA in government accommodation.
  • Transport allowance: Rs 1,350 / 900 for Levels 1 and 2, Rs 3,600 / 1,800 for Levels 3 to 8, and Rs 7,200 / 3,600 for Level 9 and above (larger cities / other places), plus DA on that amount.
  • Deductions: 10% of basic + DA for NPS or UPS, the CGHS subscription (Rs 250 for Levels 1 to 5, Rs 450 for Level 6, Rs 650 for Levels 7 to 11) and group insurance (CGEGIS).
  • Income tax: estimated under the new regime for FY 2026-27, with the Rs 75,000 standard deduction and the Section 87A rebate. The government's 14% NPS contribution is added to taxable salary and deducted again under Section 80CCD(2), so it does not change the tax.

Example, Level 4 in an X city at 63% DA: basic 25,500 + DA 16,065 + HRA 7,650 + TA 5,868 = gross Rs 55,083. Minus NPS 4,157, CGHS 250 and CGEGIS 30 gives about Rs 50,646 in hand. Our guide to in-hand salary for Levels 4 to 7 sets out the same calculation for each level with X and Z city tables.

Which Posts Are at Which Level?

As a rough guide from recent SSC notices: Level 2 for Lower Division Clerk and Junior Secretariat Assistant, Level 4 for Postal and Sorting Assistants and Tax Assistants, Level 5 for Auditors and Accountants, Level 6 for posts such as Junior Statistical Officer, and Level 7 for Assistant Section Officer and Inspector posts. The same post can sit at different levels in different departments, so always use the level printed in your notification. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the in-hand salary at Pay Level 7 in an X city?

With the starting basic of Rs 44,900 and DA at 63%, the gross is about Rs 92,525 a month (basic, DA, 30% HRA and transport allowance with DA). After NPS, CGHS and CGEGIS the in-hand pay is about Rs 84,496, and no income tax is due under the new regime at this level.

Is HRA paid if I live in government quarters?

No. Employees in government accommodation do not get HRA; a licence fee is deducted instead.

Why is income tax zero in most results?

Under the new tax regime for FY 2026-27, a salaried person with income up to Rs 12.75 lakh a year (Rs 12 lakh plus the Rs 75,000 standard deduction) pays no tax because of the Section 87A rebate. Starting salaries up to Pay Level 7 fall below this.
