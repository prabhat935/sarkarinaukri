How to Get an Image the Form Will Accept

Photograph

Use a recent colour photo against a light, preferably white, background, looking straight at the camera. Caps and dark glasses are not accepted, and if you wear glasses there should be no reflection over your eyes. Religious headwear is allowed if it does not cover the face. Use "Crop to fill" and move the frame so that your face and the top of your shoulders are centred. Bank notices advise keeping several prints of the same photo, because you paste it on the call letter and bring extra copies to the exam.

Signature

Sign on plain white paper with a black ink pen, in your usual running hand. A signature in capital letters is not accepted by IBPS and SBI. Photograph or scan it, crop away the empty paper as closely as you can, and use "Fit inside with a white border" so the whole signature stays visible. Use the same signature on the call letter and attendance sheet at the exam.

Thumb impression and declaration

Put your left thumb impression on white paper with black or blue ink. If you do not have a left thumb, the notices allow the right thumb. For the hand-written declaration, copy the exact text given in the notice in your own handwriting in English; typed or capital-letter declarations are rejected.

Why Forms Reject Images

  • File too large: phone photos are often 2 to 5 MB, far above a 50 KB limit. The tool lowers the JPEG quality step by step until the file fits.
  • File too small: a small signature can compress to 4 or 5 KB, below a 10 KB minimum. The tool adds padding data inside the JPEG file (not to the picture) to reach the minimum.
  • Wrong dimensions: the image is resized to exactly the width and height chosen.
  • Unclear image: no tool can fix a blurred or dark original. Retake the photo in daylight if the preview looks poor; the notices say unclear images can lead to rejection.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size should the photo be for IBPS and SBI forms?

The IBPS PO/MT-XVI and SBI Junior Associates 2026 notices ask for a photograph of 200 x 230 pixels (preferred) with a file size between 20 KB and 50 KB, and a signature of 140 x 60 pixels between 10 KB and 20 KB.

Why does the form say my signature is too small in KB?

A small, plain image such as a 140 x 60 signature can compress to well under 10 KB. This tool adds harmless padding data to the JPEG file so it meets the minimum size, without changing the picture.

Are my photos uploaded to your server?

No. The image is resized and compressed inside your browser. It never leaves your device.

Which format does the tool save?

JPG (JPEG), which is the format almost every government and bank application form accepts for photographs and signatures.
