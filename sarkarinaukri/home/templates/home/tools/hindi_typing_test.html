Always practise on the layout your notice names.

Setting Up a Hindi Keyboard

  • Windows: open Settings, then Time & Language, then Language & region, add Hindi, and choose the "Hindi Traditional" keyboard, which uses the InScript layout. Press Windows key + Space to switch between English and Hindi.
  • Remington (GAIL): this typewriter-based layout is not built into Windows. Install an Indic input tool that offers it, then select it in the same way.
  • Phones: phone keyboards with transliteration are fine for messages but not for exam practice. Use a computer with a physical keyboard, as in the test.

InScript, Remington and Kruti Dev

InScript is the standard Indian layout: vowels sit on the left of the keyboard and consonants on the right, and it works the same on every computer. Remington follows the old Hindi typewriter, and many people who learnt on a typewriter prefer it. Kruti Dev is a font, not a keyboard: it maps Hindi shapes onto English keys and gives text that cannot be searched or read without the font. Learn the layout your target exam allows; switching layouts later costs weeks of speed.

How Your Score Is Calculated

Only words typed exactly right count towards net speed: their characters, including the space after each word, are divided by five and by the minutes elapsed. Hindi words often contain matras and conjuncts that take several keystrokes, so the "characters per hour" figure here counts Unicode characters, which is close to but not the same as the key depressions SSC measures. Word accuracy is correct words as a share of the words you typed. The standard to aim for is 30 words per minute, about 9,000 key depressions per hour; aim for around 36 WPM in practice so you have a margin on the day. Practising English too? Frequently Asked Questions

What Hindi typing speed does SSC require?

For posts with a typing test, such as CHSL Lower Division Clerk and Junior Secretariat Assistant, the Hindi standard is 30 words per minute, about 9,000 key depressions per hour. The notice for your exam states the exact rules.

Can I practise Kruti Dev typing here?

No. Kruti Dev is a legacy font that stores English letters and only looks like Hindi with that font installed. This test works with Unicode Hindi typed through an InScript or Remington (GAIL) keyboard. If your exam requires Kruti Dev, practise in a word processor with that font as well.

How do I type Hindi on my computer for this test?

On Windows, add Hindi as a language in Settings and choose the Hindi Traditional (InScript) keyboard, or install an input tool that offers the Remington (GAIL) layout. Switch to it with Windows key + Space before you start typing.
