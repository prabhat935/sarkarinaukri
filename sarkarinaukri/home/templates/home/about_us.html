About Sarkari Naukri

Government jobs, results and exam guides, written from official notifications

What We Do

Sarkari Naukri publishes central and state government job notifications, results and admit cards for students across India, along with original guides on eligibility, reservation certificates, salary and exam preparation. We are a small independent team, and we publish fewer posts than the large job portals because each one is written in full from the recruiter's official notification rather than copied from other sites.

How We Write and Check Each Post

  • From the official notice: every job post, result and admit card page is written from the notification published by the recruiting body itself - for example ibps.in, sbi.bank.in, rrb.indianrailways.gov.in or the state commission's website. We name that source in the post and link to the official site to apply.
  • Facts checked against the notice: vacancies, eligibility, age limits and relaxations, fees, selection process and dates are checked against the official document before the post goes live. Where a notice is only in Hindi, or is a scanned copy, we read the original pages.
  • In our own words: we explain what the rules mean in practice and add tips, such as certificate requirements or common application mistakes, instead of reproducing the notice.
  • Kept current: when a last date passes, the post is marked closed automatically. If a recruiter issues a corrigendum or extension, we update the post.

What We Cover

  • Government jobs: central government bodies, public sector companies, banks, railways and state recruitment boards
  • Results and admit cards: for exams we cover, with what happens at the next stage
  • Exam guides: eligibility rules, reservation certificates (OBC, EWS), age relaxation, pay and allowances, negative marking and preparation
  • FAQ: short answers to the questions aspirants ask most often
