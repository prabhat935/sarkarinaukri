How the Calculation Works

Your age is counted in completed years, months and days on the cut-off date, the same way it is counted at document verification. A limit of 27 years means you must not have completed 28 years on that date, so a candidate whose 28th birthday falls exactly on the cut-off date is over the limit, while one whose birthday is the next day is not.

The upper limit is then raised by the relaxation for your category. Relaxation never changes the minimum age. The calculator also shows the range of birth dates that the notice allows for your category, which is how many notices state the limit ("born not before ... and not later than ...").

For ex-servicemen, the calculator uses the rule found in SSC and most Group C notices: the military service you have rendered is deducted from your actual age, and the result may exceed the upper limit by 3 years. Ex-servicemen who are OBC or SC/ST usually get 3 or 5 more years on top of that (6 and 8 years in total). Gazetted posts filled through UPSC have different ex-servicemen rules, so check the notice. The calculator does not combine ex-servicemen and disability relaxation, because notices treat that case separately.

A Worked Example

An SSC-style post has an age limit of 18 to 27 years on 1 January 2027. Riya, an OBC candidate, was born on 15 March 1997. On 1 January 2027 she is 29 years, 9 months and 17 days old. Her upper limit is 27 + 3 = 30 years, and she has not completed 30 years on the cut-off date, so she is eligible. The birth date range for OBC candidates is 2 January 1996 to 1 January 2009.

Arjun, an ex-serviceman in the unreserved category, is 38 years and 2 months old on the same date and served 17 years and 6 months. After deducting his service his age is 20 years and 8 months, which is within the 30-year limit (27 + 3) that applies to him.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do EWS candidates get age relaxation in central government jobs?

No. EWS candidates have reserved vacancies but follow the same upper age limit as unreserved candidates in central government recruitment.

Is the disability relaxation added to the SC, ST or OBC relaxation?

Yes. In central recruitment the 10 years for a benchmark disability is added to the category relaxation: 10 years for unreserved and EWS, 13 for OBC and 15 for SC and ST candidates.

On which date is my age counted?

On the crucial date given in the notification, not on the day you apply. SSC usually fixes a date such as 1 January or 1 August; UPSC recruitment advertisements usually use the closing date.

If I turn 28 on the cut-off date, am I within a limit of 27 years?

No. A limit of 27 years means you must not have completed 28 years on the cut-off date. If your 28th birthday falls on that date, you are over the limit.

Does this calculator work for state government jobs?

It calculates the age correctly for any date, but its relaxation figures are the usual central government ones. State jobs often have different relaxations, so for those enter the upper limit for your category from the notice and choose Unreserved.
