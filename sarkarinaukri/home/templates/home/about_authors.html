About Our Editorial Team

Who writes the guides on Sarkari Naukri, and how we check them.

Sarkari Naukri is a small, independent site run by people who follow government recruitment closely. We started it because official notifications are long and hard to read, and the important details, like the exact date your age is counted on or which certificate format is accepted, are easy to miss. Our aim is to explain those details in plain language and point you back to the official source.

Sarkari Naukri Editorial Team

Sarkari Naukri Editorial Team

Guides and job information, checked against official notifications

Our guides are written and reviewed by the team as a whole rather than under individual bylines. Before a guide is published, its dates, eligibility rules and salary figures are checked against the official notification, pay matrix or government order it relies on. Where a figure depends on something that changes, such as the Dearness Allowance rate, we say which value we used and show how to recalculate it.

What we cover: how to read notifications, eligibility and age rules, exam patterns, salary and allowances, and the application and document verification process.

Our Commitment to Quality

Editorial Standards

  • All content verified against official government sources (SSC, UPSC, RRB, Banking bodies, etc.)
  • Guides updated when a notification, rule or rate they depend on changes
  • Every guide shows when it was last updated
  • Original explanations written for this site, not copied from other portals

Information Sources

  • Official government organization websites (SSC, UPSC, RRB, IBPS, SBI, etc.)
  • Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT) orders and the 7th CPC pay matrix
  • Public Service Commission notifications and circulars
  • Banking and insurance regulatory body announcements
  • Verified media reports and official government press releases

Why You Can Trust Us

Independence & Transparency: Sarkari Naukri is an independent resource portal with NO affiliation to any government organization. We do not represent SSC, UPSC, RRB, IBPS, SBI, or any other recruiting body. Our role is purely informational — to help you understand the recruitment process and access official information more easily.

Accuracy: We check eligibility rules, salary figures and dates against official sources before publishing a guide. Mistakes can still happen, so always confirm against the official notification before you apply, and tell us if you spot an error.

User Focus: Our content is written for government job aspirants. We prioritize clarity and practical advice over promotions.

Corrections: When a reader reports an error, or a rule changes, we update the guide and its "last updated" date.

Common Questions

  • Is Sarkari Naukri affiliated with any government body? No. We are an independent information portal. See our disclaimer for details.
  • Where does job information come from? From the official notifications published by recruiting organizations. Always check the official notification before applying.
  • Can I apply for jobs directly through your site? No. You must apply through official government websites. We provide information and guidance only.
  • How often is content updated? New guides are added regularly, and existing guides are revised when the rules they cover change.
  • Do you provide coaching? No. We publish free guides and explanations only.
