Contact Us

Sarkari Naukri is run by a small editorial team that writes every job post, result and admit card page from the official notification. If something on the site is wrong or unclear, we want to hear about it.

Email

support@sarkarinaukriresult.online

We read every message and usually reply within two working days.

What you can write to us about

  • A mistake in a post - a wrong date, fee, age limit or vacancy count. Please include the page link and, if you can, the official notice that shows the correct detail. We check it and correct the page.
  • A notification we have missed - send the recruiter's name or a link to the official notice and we will consider writing it up.
  • Questions about the site - how we choose and check what we publish, privacy, or advertising on the site.

Please note: we are not a government body and are not linked to any recruiting organisation. We cannot accept job applications, change your application, or tell you your result. For those, contact the recruiting body through the official website named in each post.
